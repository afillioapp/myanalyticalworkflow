# Practice: the SABS change, worked as a BSA

Started 2026-11-11. Purpose: learn the real BSA job on a real regulatory change, and find where the app helps or falls short at each stage.

**The scene.** You are the BSA at a mid-size Ontario auto insurer (fictional, the same one as the sample model). It is July 2025. FSRA has just published the filing specifications (C-03). Your manager forwards the FSRA announcement (C-01) with one line: "We need to be ready for this. Can you take it?"

**Source material:** the P1 corpus, C-01 to C-05 and C-10 to C-12, listed in `research/corpus/sources.md`. Facts in this file come from those documents. Anything not in them is marked as an assumption.

**How it works.** One stage at a time. Each stage ends in an outcome (rule 16). After each stage: what the app does there today, and what it should do.

| # | Stage | Outcome | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake | Trigger, sponsor, deadline and reason, scope in and out, affected areas, constraints | In progress |
| 2 | Understand today | The current-state picture for the affected areas | Not started |
| 3 | Impact | The list of what the change touches, and why | Not started |
| 4 | Discovery | Answers from stakeholders, and the open questions | Not started |
| 5 | Options and to-be | The chosen option, the future state, the gaps | Not started |
| 6 | Requirements | Business requirements, rules and acceptance criteria | Not started |
| 7 | Traceability | Every requirement traced to an obligation, nothing orphaned | Not started |
| 8 | Sign-off | What gets approved, and by whom | Not started |
